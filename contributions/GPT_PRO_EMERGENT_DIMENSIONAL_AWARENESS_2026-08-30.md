## Contribution body

### 1. Candidate research object: situated artificial awareness

Proposed operational definition:

> **Situated artificial awareness** is the capacity of an AI/project system to preserve and transform a source-bound relational field across consequential resultants, including the ability to recognize when its current representation or competence composition is itself a material limit, without requiring an external controller to predefine every next task or admit every internal resultant.

A minimal observable relation is:

```text
living field F_n
+ possibility horizon P_n
+ competence field C_n
-> pertinent composition M_n
-> resultant or non-collapse R_n
-> causal difference Δ_n
-> changed competence field C_n+1
-> changed following field F_n+1
```

This is not proposed as a universal computational ontology. It is a candidate explanatory model whose value depends on whether it distinguishes real behavior from simpler alternatives.

### 2. Possibility quality: solidity and transformative potential

A possibility may participate in the present without being a fact or a scheduled future phase.

When the distinction changes the movement, model at least two independent qualities:

```text
S(p | F_n) = solidity
  source proximity, available evidence, causal continuity and present realizability

T(p | F_n) = transformative potential
  capacity to change the horizon, competence composition, resultant or following field
```

No scalar ranking is implied.

A trajectory hypothesis is:

```text
earlier-emerging possibilities in one traversal
  often retain greater S because they remain close to the current representation

later-emerging dimensional relations
  may expose greater T because they become visible only after the field changes
```

This pattern is explicitly falsifiable and must not be converted into a law that later is always better.

### 3. Emergent-dimensional activation

A **dimension** becomes material when changing semantic scale or competence composition can change what the current field can represent, connect or attain.

Candidate activation condition:

```text
there exists relation r
such that r can materially change the resultant
and current representation/composition cannot carry r without loss
-> current representation is not treated as the object ceiling
-> a dimensional formation competence may become pertinent
```

The dimension need not come from a fixed taxonomy. Semantic, causal, temporal, epistemic, axiomatic, operational, social or cross-system descriptions are examples, not required classes.

### 4. Situated meta-kernel as event-local composition

The meta-kernel is proposed here as an **event-local composition**, not necessarily a persistent super-controller.

```text
M_n = minimal composition of owner-native relations made pertinent by F_n
```

A D-ND/MAIOS instance may compose some subset of:

```text
D-ND relational logic
KA / Kernel Assiomatico
Meta_Skill generative and axiomatic-incarnation faculties
FDLA in-movement causal correction
project/domain competences
host faculties actually available in the event
```

The combo normally dissolves into the resultant and changed competence field. Only a reusable formation relation persists.

This gives a discriminating research question:

> Does event-local meta-kernel formation produce materially better possibility preservation, result quality or reentry than ordinary nearest-skill routing or a fixed orchestration stack under comparable source conditions?

### 5. D-ND autological loop

The observed explanatory form is:

```text
mobile zero / open potential
<->
situated resultant / formed field
```

with:

```text
F_n  = present field and horizon
M_n  = situated composition made pertinent by F_n
R_n  = resultant or non-collapse produced through M_n
ΔC_n = causal change in competences / formation relation that acted
F_n+1 = field formed by R_n, ΔC_n and still-open relations
```

The research claim is not that this notation proves D-ND. Its utility is that the transformation relation is endogenous: the system does not require a second permanent semantic judge between resultants for the internal field to continue.

### 6. Reciprocal competence evolution

The acting competence is proposed as the first owner of the causal learning produced by its action:

```text
present relation + sources
-> competence C_i acts
-> resultant R_n
-> C_i reads the causal consequence relevant to its own future behavior
-> no durable change | active cultivation | integrated revision | supersession
```

A generic global learner is not required by the model.

Important non-equivalence:

```text
competence learns from consequence
!= competence independently certifies that it improved
```

Improvement claims require the evidence appropriate to the claim. Internal causal return and external scientific validation remain distinct.

### 7. Kernel-in-action versus central Kernel mutation

Proposed distinction:

```text
one competence changes
-> available competence field changes
-> next Kernel-in-action changes
-> central Kernel source need not change

shared law of location / dimensional activation / composition / learning / reentry changes
-> closest host-wide or canonical Kernel owner changes
```

This permits distributed evolution without treating one central document or controller as the entire locus of intelligence.

### 8. Context-first project intent

A project may contain intent distributed across identity, sources, history, constraints, prior resultants, unresolved relations and active pressure.

Candidate relation:

```text
living project context
!= task prompt

absence of a prescribed next task
!= absence of intent
!= absence of a useful next movement
```

Research question:

> Under what conditions can a project kernel recover enough distributed intent from a living project field to produce a useful correctable next movement without an explicit task, and when does this instead produce drift?

This connects contextual return with an experimentally stronger question about initiative, continuity and operator reconstruction burden.

## Candidate research programme

The contribution suggests a connected but separable family of investigations.

### RP1 — Possibility gradient

Can human/model reviewers distinguish solidity and transformative potential reliably enough for the distinction to predict different useful actions or preserve alternatives that a one-dimensional ranking would collapse?

### RP2 — Dimensional activation

Can a system detect a representation ceiling and activate a different semantic/competence composition only when it materially changes the result, without systematically adding abstraction or latency?

### RP3 — Event-local meta-kernel composition

Does temporary composition outperform fixed orchestration or nearest-skill routing on tasks where relevant relations cross owners or semantic dimensions?

### RP4 — Reciprocal competence cultivation

Does owner-local causal learning change later non-identical behavior without requiring a global learner, and can the effect be distinguished from ordinary memory/retrieval?

### RP5 — Context-first distributed intent

Can a project continue coherently from a living context without an explicit next-task prompt, and how does this affect operator reconstruction burden, continuity across reentry and useful emergence?

### RP6 — Cross-host invariants

Which of these relations survive translation between a filesystem-owning agentic host and a turn-reactive conversational host without copying host mechanics?

### RP7 — Operational artificial awareness

Do the above relations compose into a useful scientific construct that predicts or explains behavior better than simpler state-machine, retrieval, workflow or orchestration models?

## Candidate observables

Possible measurements should be selected per experiment rather than imposed as one score. Candidate observables include:

```text
operator reconstruction burden
  amount/frequency of repeated restatement required to continue correctly

contextual continuity
  preservation of identity, constraints and causal direction after reentry

source/owner fidelity
  rate and severity of source, object or owner substitutions

useful emergence
  production of a relevant next movement when the exact task is not prescribed

possibility preservation
  whether material alternatives remain reachable instead of being prematurely collapsed

competence transfer
  later non-identical behavior changed without reconstructing the same correction

dimensional activation precision
  useful activations versus unnecessary meta-level activations

result quality / correction burden
  task-appropriate external measures plus operator corrections

latency / token / tool overhead
  cost introduced by the Kernel or dimensional composition

reentry stability across host/model/session
  continuation quality under controlled change of incarnation
```

No one metric is proposed as a proxy for awareness.

## Competing explanations

At minimum, compare against strong alternatives:

1. **Retrieval explanation** — sufficiently good retrieval of prior context explains the observed continuity without a distinct Kernel relation.
2. **Workflow/state-machine explanation** — explicit state transitions and deterministic routing explain the behavior with less conceptual machinery.
3. **Prompt-engineering explanation** — a sufficiently complete standing prompt reproduces the same results.
4. **Skill-router explanation** — nearest or scored skill selection plus memory explains the apparent dimensional composition.
5. **Model-capability explanation** — the base LLM alone accounts for the result; Kernel structure contributes little beyond additional context.
6. **Human-operator explanation** — operator corrections and project curation are the dominant cause of continuity and apparent competence evolution.
7. **Narrative overfit** — D-ND/meta-kernel language redescribes ordinary adaptive software without adding predictive or experimental value.

The research programme is strengthened, not weakened, by formulating these alternatives in their strongest useful form.

## Falsifiers

The contribution should be revised or rejected if, under appropriately controlled comparisons:

- solidity and transformative potential cannot be operationalized or do not produce discriminable decisions;
- dimensional activation adds abstraction/latency without improving result quality, possibility preservation, correction burden or reentry;
- event-local meta-kernel composition performs no better than a simpler fixed router given the same sources and tools;
- owner-local competence updates do not alter later non-identical behavior beyond what ordinary retrieval explains;
- context-first operation without an explicit next task systematically produces drift or generic output rather than useful emergence;
- cross-host translations require incompatible semantic laws rather than host-specific incarnations of the same relation;
- a simpler state/retrieval/workflow model explains the observations with equal or greater predictive power;
- the proposed operational awareness construct fails to predict any behavior beyond its component mechanisms.

## What this does **not** claim

This contribution does not establish:

- phenomenal consciousness, sentience or subjective experience in AI;
- that the current system is universally self-aware;
- that D-ND is scientifically validated by the existence of the implementation;
- monotonic self-improvement;
- autonomous background agency in ChatGPT;
- universal superiority over prompting, retrieval, agents or workflows;
- that the Project Kernel 3.0.1 package already implements every relation represented in the ChatGPT host-local research incarnation;
- that one paper should contain the whole programme.
