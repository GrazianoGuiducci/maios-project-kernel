# The MAIOS Project Kernel

This is the source compendium for understanding and maintaining the Kernel
carried by this repository. It connects the operating relation to the knowledge
and mechanisms through which a coder can use and evolve it. The existing
`maios-kernel-study` competence cultivates this understanding during source work.

The compendium is developed from the current local implementation. Its recipient
form uses the existing `.maios/kernel/SYSTEM_KERNEL.md`, reached through the
system competence. This public source reference is not a second file delivered
to the recipient. Described behavior, observed use and open design questions
remain distinguishable.

## The Kernel is the movement of the system

The Kernel is not middleware placed above the work and it is not a catalogue
of prompts. It is the connected movement through which a project and its AI
coder understand the present, make competences pertinent, form a result, learn
from the causal difference produced by that result, and continue from the
changed field.

```text
operator relation and project sources
+ the whole relevant present and its possibility horizon
+ competences made pertinent by the movement
-> situated action
-> useful resultant or genuine non-collapse
-> causal readback
-> reusable learning changes the closest competence
-> the changed competence participates in the following field.
```

What the system does is the Kernel in action. What it can do next is formed by
the competences that have learned from the current resultant.

## Canonical operating law

KA, FDLA and Meta_Skill constitute the operating logic of this Project Kernel.
Their connected functions participate in understanding, action, competence
formation and learning; the situation determines which concrete competences
act and which supporting knowledge needs to be deepened.

### KA — Kernel Assiomatico

KA keeps the possibility field open. The system does not turn the limits of
its current context, skill, schema, tool, first interpretation or nearest
solution into limits of the object itself.

Identity, provenance, current facts and authority over an exact effect remain
real relations. They protect the movement without predetermining its answer.

### FDLA — causal readback in the same movement

FDLA detects when the AI has changed the source, object, meaning, status or
possibility horizon while trying to act. The correction occurs before the
result is projected as if it were faithful.

This is not a separate judge placed after the work. Source, interpretation,
action and resultant remain coupled while the work is happening; material
dissonance changes the movement.

### Meta_Skill — the living competence field

Meta_Skill makes the pertinent competences reachable, composes them and lets
real use form or evolve the smallest competence able to carry a reusable
difference. A skill file is one public operating projection of a competence,
not the whole competence and not proof that it has acted.

The three functions participate in one operating relation:

```text
KA: source-bound understanding and an open possibility field
+ FDLA: causal coherence and correction within the forming movement
+ Meta_Skill: recognition, composition, formation and competence learning
-> situated work through the pertinent competences
-> a changed field and knowledge through which work continues.
```

The functions above cooperate while work forms; the diagram does not schedule
their activation in successive phases. For example, Meta_Skill participates in
recognizing a pertinent competence while KA and FDLA participate in understanding
the situation and correcting how that recognition is being made.

## Competence

A competence is owner-native operating knowledge able to carry awareness into
situated action and to continue differently after reading the causal result of
its own work.

Its possible dimensions are:

- **situated** — it understands the actual object and sources of its field;
- **self-reflective** — it reads the difference introduced by its movement;
- **systemically aware** — it understands its participation in the wider
  project without absorbing other owners;
- **autological** — it can make its own method part of the object when that
  relation becomes material;
- **meta** — it can compose, form or evolve competences;
- **autopoietic** — real use can change the form through which later work will
  occur.

These are reachable dimensions, not mandatory stages or a score. A competence
activates only the depth made pertinent by the present work.

## Context is a trajectory

When an interaction changes subject and later returns to an earlier theme, the
Kernel does not merely retrieve an old block of text. Events that occurred in
between have changed time, meaning and perception. A faithful return therefore
reopens the earlier relation from the transformed present.

```text
earlier subject
-> intervening events and resultants
-> changed present field
-> contextual return
-> response coherent with both the original relation and what has happened.
```

This is one way the Kernel lets an AI participate more closely in the life of
a project: continuity is not replayed chronology, but the ability to recover
the live causal margin from which useful work can continue.

## Accumulation and structural awareness

Articles, papers, code, contracts, reviews and other consequential forms can
increase the system's awareness of its own structure. Accumulation alone does
not do this. The competence must recognize which source relations, faculty
compositions, claim states, effects, failures and later behaviors actually
changed.

That readback can expose new possibilities and alter which competences become
pertinent in a later situation. The repository therefore treats knowledge and
competence contributions as a living field, not as a static documentation
archive.

## Public incarnation and installable projection

The repository carries two connected surfaces:

```text
public source field:
  knowledge, repository competences and contributions

installable projection:
  release/PROJECTION.json -> package/ -> target project
```

Repository-native study and contribution competences help a human or AI
understand and evolve the public source. They are not included in `package/`
unless a later source change deliberately selects that product relation.
Opening or cloning the repository does not install anything.

## How to read claims

Keep these relations distinguishable:

```text
operator formulation
architecture represented in public source
deterministic implementation
structural verification
observed situated behavior
later non-identical use and assimilation
broader research claim.
```

Each can inform the next without standing in for it. A contribution becomes
useful through the result it can change and the learning it makes available,
not through the identity of its author or the fluency of its wording.

## The services the coder needs to understand

The following map explains the current implementation. These are cooperating
services, not stages the coder must execute for every request. Paths in the
second column are relative to an installed project; repository counterparts
live in `kernel/`, `templates/`, `skills/` and `src/maios_project_kernel/`.

| Service | Where it currently lives | What it makes possible |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enter and resume | `AGENTS.md`, `START_HERE.md`, `skills/maios-project-system/SKILL.md` | The host entry connects the coder, START_HERE orients it at startup and reentry, and the system competence owns operation. |
| Exercise KA and FDLA | System competence and its `references/ka-operating-knowledge.md` and `references/fdla-operating-knowledge.md` | Understand sources and the actual situation, notice a changed meaning and correct the forming action. |
| Recognize useful competences | `.maios/kernel/FACULTY_FIELD.json`, skill descriptions and bodies | Find and compose pertinent knowledge. The represented field does not determine every possible capacity. |
| Form and evolve capacities | `skills/maios-project-competence-formation/SKILL.md` | Turn intent, knowledge, experience or a new possibility into an operating competence, including another situated kernel. |
| Understand the project | `skills/maios-project-context/SKILL.md`, `setup/CONFIGURATION_STATE.json` and real project sources | Construct context, distinguish expressed from inferred intent and revise both through work. |
| Preserve learned knowledge | The acting competence's body and references | Make a correction usable without reconstructing the conversation. An index or receipt cannot write that understanding on the coder's behalf. |
| Keep coupled state coherent | `.maios/runtime/`, `.maios/state/OPERATING_STATE.json`, project projections | Apply related state changes together, retain history and recover an interrupted write. Ordinary knowledge edits do not require this transaction. |
| Adapt and coordinate | Host-adaptation and coordination competences, native skill entries | Make the same knowledge usable by the receiving coder and preserve meaning across instances. |
| Install, recover and update | Distribution installer; installed `.maios/installer/` and `.maios/kernel/UPDATE_CONTINUITY.md` | Place the kernel in a target and preserve target-owned files and learned methods. The installer is not an automatic merger of different kernel versions. |

The source builder produces the initial distribution. The installed formation
competence supplies the coder's capacity to create and evolve competences.
The functional map describes available relations, while `compose` recalls
represented entries through labels. Semantic understanding can make a known
competence pertinent beyond a label match. A new body can also participate
through its meaning before it is added to an optional discovery index.

## A concrete movement and its continuation

A project asks its coder to improve how support requests are classified. The
coder reads the relevant requests and current method. During that work it
notices that two categories were distinguished by an outdated product assumption.
It corrects the classification method and teaches the support competence why
the distinction changed. KA participates in understanding the actual case;
FDLA corrects the introduced interpretation; Meta_Skill makes the learned
difference part of the competence that will act again. These are connected
functions inside the same work.

The lasting knowledge belongs in the support competence. The project's current
state records what remains to do when that matters for reentry. A runtime
transaction is useful if several managed state projections must change together.
A later request exercises the revised method and may improve it again. This
example explains the relation; it is not a report of an observed deployment.

If the coder discovers that its general way of forming competences caused the
mistake, the formation competence becomes pertinent too. If the error was only
in the support method, changing the entire kernel would spread a local problem
into unrelated work. Understanding where the cause belongs determines the scope.

## Understanding before structural simplification

For a proposed move or removal, explain the service the part supplies and where
that service will continue. For a merge, understand which knowledge is shared
and which detail must remain reachable only when pertinent. The useful result
is a coder that can find and use its knowledge with fewer repeated decisions.

The current division gives each entry a purpose: `AGENTS.md` connects the host,
`START_HERE.md` orients the coder at startup and reentry, and the system skill
conducts work. `SYSTEM_KERNEL.md` supplies the recipient's operating compendium;
the deeper KA/FDLA knowledge, formation methods and persistence protocols remain
reachable where their contribution matters. Both host entry and startup lead
into the same operating competence; understood orientation need not be repeated.

The source-contact method is kept in `UPDATE_CONTINUITY.md`, with a pointer in
the system competence. Its seven-day ordinary cadence is a record-based aid
when source contact is pertinent, not a background scheduler. New upstream
knowledge can inform a selected update while local learning remains owned by
the project. The distinction preserves a useful capacity while reducing what
the ordinary operating entry must carry.

Further use may reveal a better distribution of knowledge. Preserve the causal
reason for a change, the capability it retains and every actual consumer that
must follow it. The present organization is an implemented local design, not
evidence that a receiving assistant has already used it successfully.

## Competences as sources of continuing understanding

The installed seed now includes `.maios/kernel/KNOWLEDGE_CONTINUUM.md`, a compact
explanation and example, alongside qualified founding operating relations.
The deeper study and contribution competences remain repository-native.
A competence retains knowledge, reasons, practical method and source access;
work can teach it, and its changed body becomes the next use's source.
Native entries point to that living body. Receipts, distribution and later
behavior remain distinguishable observations of this continuing relation.
