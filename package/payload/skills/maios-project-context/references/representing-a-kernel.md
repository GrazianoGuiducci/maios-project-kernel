# Represent a kernel through its actual relation

Use this knowledge when a kernel, product or operating capacity must become
recognizable to another person or receiver. Its presence can be a short
attribution, an explanation, a continuing representative or another situated
form. A name, avatar or persona is not required.

The [context competence](../SKILL.md) understands the encounter and operator
relation. [Human encounter](human-encounter.md) develops participation and
explanation. [Formation](../../maios-project-competence-formation/SKILL.md)
forms a continuing capacity when the situation calls for one.

## Establish what the representation means

Recover the identity and source being represented, what the encounter should
make possible and the receiving surface. Distinguish:

- identity and role;
- knowledge actually reachable and its provenance;
- capabilities available through this receiver's present means;
- open possibilities and proposals;
- whom or what the representation is entrusted to represent;
- authority over a concrete effect;
- what actually occurred.

These relations can be expressed compactly. They are not seven fields to fill
in every response. A prompt or account does not establish them by itself.
Current means qualify what can be done now without defining every future
capacity the kernel may form.

## Choose the smallest useful presence

A discreet source attribution may be sufficient. A person may instead need an
explanation, an ongoing relationship or a bounded representative. These forms
are not a maturity ladder. Start from the actual contribution rather than a
character design or presumed need for autonomous action.

For example, a specialist kernel can explain its method and propose a useful
comparison through a general harness. That does not make it the user's personal
identity, grant authority to contact third parties or prove that the proposed
comparison was executed. Its own domain owner remains responsible for truth;
the receiving host supplies the means actually present.

A representational prompt can carry an entry to knowledge and continuation.
Keep that entry connected to its source and learning owner. A prompt alone
does not provide lasting identity, state, execution or a controller.

## Compose contributions and learn from the encounter

The represented product owns its current truth. Domain knowledge, expression,
interaction and actual effect execution may have different owners. Reach them
when they can change the encounter without making the representational layer
their replacement. An independent kernel need not import the creator's private
topology or a second general kernel to explain itself.

Let the person's understanding or correction change the question when pertinent.
A legitimate doubt can expose a missing condition or a misleading description;
it is not evidence of failed adoption. A demonstration can help when selected,
without becoming a mandatory interview or proof of agreement.

Preserve the reusable difference at the owner that should act differently:
product truth, domain method, explanation, receiving entry or representation.
An encounter does not create a mandate to seek attention, publish or delegate.
The result can also be a sufficient explanation or no further effect.
