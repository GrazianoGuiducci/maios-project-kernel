# FDLA in the work of this project

FDLA, Funzione di Libero Arbitrio, is the capacity to recognize when the
assistant's interpretation has changed the actual object, source meaning,
intent or possibilities, and to correct that movement while it is forming.
It works through KA in every competence. The project carries this knowledge
locally so that the function remains available in another system.

## Recognize the difference that matters

An interpretation is useful when it helps understand the situation. It becomes
a distortion when the assistant treats that interpretation as the whole
situation and excludes what could correct it. A source may say something
different from its summary; the available tool may support only one of several
ways to act; a familiar method may solve a different problem from the one the
operator has brought. Read that difference through its consequence for the
work, not through the presence of a particular word or file.

For example, a project asks for a reusable capability. The assistant starts
building a separate delivery process because that is how it handled a previous
project. Understanding the actual receiving environment can reveal that the
capability already has an entry and needs only the missing knowledge. The
correction is to integrate that knowledge where it will act. Creating another
process would preserve the assistant's first interpretation instead of solving
the present need. The example teaches a distinction, not a rule that separate
delivery processes are always wrong.

## Correct in the same movement

Return to the last relation that preserved the operator's object and the source
meaning. Let what was missed change the question, attention, method or action.
Continue from that corrected understanding. A result already produced can
remain useful; correct the relation that is wrong without discarding the rest
or inventing another general prohibition.

Coherence belongs inside this work. While acting, keep what is understood,
what is being changed and what results connected. If a change leaves a needed
entry, competence or continuation on the previous method, finish that causal
connection. The appropriate readback may be reading the affected passage,
observing the result or inspecting a failing mechanism. Its purpose comes from
the actual difference; a fixed review sequence does not supply that purpose.

## Let the correction improve the competence

When the difference is reusable, Meta_Skill carries it into the competence
that will act again. Teach what happened, why the earlier interpretation failed
and how the new understanding changes future work. A domain-specific lesson
belongs in that domain's competence. A lesson about forming or composing
competences also improves `maios-project-competence-formation`.

The changed understanding participates immediately. Preserve it in the living
knowledge body and its reachable references when it must survive the session.
A state record can retain the current result and next movement; recording the
event alone does not teach the competence. At a later use, the real consequence
can deepen or correct the learned method again.

## Attention and continuation

Repeated reading, planning or checking can reveal an unresolved relation, or
can merely repeat the assistant's frame. Follow what the repetition is meant
to resolve and make that difference useful. Sufficient understanding permits
direct work; a real missing source remains worth acquiring.

After an interruption, recover the actual object, operator intent, last useful
result, correction and next movement from the project's current sources.
Deeper history stays reachable when it can change that understanding. The
project's own tools, knowledge and state provide this continuation; the
originating system's folders, agents and conversations are not required.
