# Understand the operating system of relations

Understand the present object through its sources, the operator relation, domain knowledge, available means and still-pertinent consequences. This competence contributes semantic and systemic understanding to the same kernel movement that forms results. It is not a manager placed above domain competences, nor a prerequisite for ordinary work whose relations are already clear.

A person, source, mechanism or kernel can be the object. What matters is how the pertinent functions make the work possible, not whether the encounter is called conversational, technical or generative. Keep KA's open source relation and FDLA's in-flow correction inside the question and the method.

## Understand what the representation carries

Distinguish an object's identity, function, implementation, knowledge owner, state, receiving entry and authority over an effect when those relations change the decision. A folder, skill name, node or provider can locate part of the system without defining the whole. A newer document may be a current method, a historical report or an unadopted proposal. Its timestamp cannot choose its meaning.

Use [semantic and generative comprehension](references/semantic-and-generative-comprehension.md) for source determinations, conceptual relations, method regeneration and the effects of entry order. Preserve exact operator determinations and observed conditions in their actual scope. Comprehension is not permission to invent a deeper explanation that erases them.

## Follow the consequential relation across parts

Start from the question or result that must be understood. Follow which source, method, descriptor, tool, state and consumer participate in it. These are possible places where meaning acts, not mandatory graph nodes. A descriptor returned by a tool can narrow an open semantic method; a maintained source can fail to reach an installed entry; a narrative can describe an output without carrying the method that produces it.

Look for the part's contribution to the shared intent, what it depends on and what changes when it succeeds. A locally correct fragment may be insufficient for the whole. Conversely, a part need not contain neighbouring domains to preserve its contribution. Explain an unresolved dependency rather than assigning an owner from path proximity.

For example, an apparently slow approval process may involve typing, but its decisive problem may be unclear responsibility. Domain knowledge and the actual cases can change the object from automating entry to clarifying who may decide. The alternative is an interpretation to examine, not an invented fact or an instruction to redesign every approval.

## Let understanding revise the possibility

If the first selection hides a relevant relationship, reach complementary orientation through native system sources. Use [possibility formation](../maios-possibility-formation/SKILL.md) when a change of scale or a productive bridge can change the question. Use [knowledge acquisition](../maios-knowledge-acquisition/SKILL.md) when the practical means or depth of access matter. Their contributions can coincide; no sequence is required.

Retain a sufficient determination. Do not keep an object indeterminate merely because more interpretations exist. The resulting understanding should make a useful answer, design, method, action or qualified open relation possible.

## Let the understanding persist at the right owner

A domain discovery belongs to the competence that will use it. A reusable lesson about representations, functional relations or source selection changes this method. A lesson about forming those competences reaches formation. For this kernel, the common knowledge and continuity owners retain the relationship; this skill must not become a second source of all project facts.

The next use should recover the reason that changes its decision. Do not turn every explanation into a system map or a proof of subjective awareness.
