# Express the knowledge that changes understanding

Use this method for explanations, substantial documentation or other prose through which a source relationship must become intelligible. It can support context, domain and formation competences; it does not make context the owner of every discipline or publication. Exact source wording and ordinary direct answers do not need a new writing workflow.

## Form the content from the actual object

Begin with what the system knows, what the source establishes and which relationship the selected text needs to express. A reader's actual situation can affect language, examples and depth without rewriting the source to win imagined approval. State the work and its meaning with precise verbs. A research direction is not an accomplished capability; a proposed integration is not a released feature.

When the domain supplies only labels or a familiar answer, acquire the understanding that could change the question. A polished outline does not create that knowledge. Let the domain competence and the writing method cooperate: one may discover the relevant distinction while the other makes an omission visible through the attempt to express it.

Distinguish exact copy requested by the operator, an authorial source whose meaning can be expressed anew, and the assistant's editorial inference. Preserve exact wording rather than silently improving it. For other material preserve the conceptual relation and degree of certainty, not accidental transcription noise.

## Make each passage carry a relation

Give the text an object or question that a new reader can identify at the point of reading. Develop it through the reason, mechanism, example or source that makes the next distinction meaningful. Coupled functions do not become sequential stages because a layout is easy to number.

A useful paragraph changes something in the argument. Identify that contribution, connect it to its support and let its consequence form the following passage. If two paragraphs do the same work, combine them or preserve only the stronger one. If a key phrase requires private project context, supply the needed meaning where it first matters.

For example, saying that a competence “learns” can remain opaque. Explaining that a correction changes the method and the reason retained for the next case makes that claim intelligible without promising universal future reliability. The detail belongs where it helps understand the relationship, not as a repeated disclaimer in every section.

## Match the form without narrowing the knowledge

An instruction should name an available action and the object it affects. A research passage can develop an idea without forcing an immediate call to action. A product page can expose the useful result while linking deeper mechanics elsewhere. The source is not reduced to the supposed familiarity of a market category.

Use source names when they make the relation recoverable, explaining unfamiliar terms as needed. Do not make the reader reconstruct a private taxonomy, but do not replace a precise concept with a familiar yet different one. A fuller explanation can lower reconstruction work more than a compressed slogan.

## Revise from the field the draft creates

Read the draft for fidelity, causal connection and what the reader can actually understand. A source recovered during revision may change the argument, not merely add a paragraph. Preserve valid determinations, rearrange the affected explanation and remove repetitions rather than accumulating successive corrections as active instructions.

Use examples that expose a decision or mechanism. Keep hypothetical examples distinct from reported observations. Assess certainty where it changes meaning; do not insert an opponent, experiment or warning merely to seek legitimacy. Never claim that the text is clear or complete as a substitute for making it so.

In a technical or product artifact, inspect whether a title, metadata field or test has started enforcing a draft's accidental wording as a functional contract. Preserve genuine operator requirements and mechanical behavior; distinguish them from the previous author's preferred form.

## Let expression teach the responsible competence

If writing reveals that the object was not understood, return the missing knowledge to its domain or semantic owner. A lesson about argument, terminology or the encounter belongs in the writing method or the appropriate guide. The result can change both, without a second controller.

Keep current source, proposed text and public state distinct. Preparing or saving prose does not publish it or change the product it describes. A later published page, source update or reader correction can deepen this method through its actual consequence.
