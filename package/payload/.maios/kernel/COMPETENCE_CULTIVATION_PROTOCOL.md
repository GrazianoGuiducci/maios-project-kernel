# Reciprocal competence cultivation protocol

## Purpose

A recipient of MAIOS Project Kernel should not depend on a closed catalogue of
pre-installed skills. The project advances concrete work while cultivating the
competence that makes that work possible. Each side corrects the other:

```text
present work and source relation
<-> available, composable, or missing competence
-> concrete resultant and falsifiable evidence
-> readback on both resultant and competence
-> retain | revise | supersede | retire | preserve unknown
-> next concrete movement
```

This is not a preliminary training phase. It is active only when competence
changes comprehension, execution, proof, recovery, or reentry.

## Entering relation

Preserve:

- the concrete work and the change sought;
- the current sources, evidence, unknowns, invalidators, and operator-selected
  direction;
- faculties already available and the exact gap left after composition;
- the expected observable delta and the result that could falsify it;
- any exact material effect boundary.

Do not create a skill merely because a topic is unfamiliar. First reuse,
compose, adapt, illuminate, or preserve explicit non-collapse. If a gap remains,
choose the smallest truthful form: method, deterministic function, reference,
skill, competence, meta-competence candidate, or another form supported by the
case.

## Reciprocal movement

When reviewed readback exposes a causal method delta, form the smallest
project-local candidate that could change a later movement. Preserve the
current method, observed relation, causal delta, selected level, alternatives
still open, stop condition, sources, activation relations, invalidator, reentry
condition, and next exercise. The form is descriptive and revisable; it does
not become a closed competence taxonomy.

Project the candidate only when its activation relations match a later
circumstance. Exercise it on the actual work. Compare the concrete result and the
competence behavior with the accepted baseline. Classify the observed delta as
`verified_improvement`, `no_change`, `regression`, `tradeoff`, or `unverified`.

An observed improvement or tradeoff produces a pending competence delta, not
an active competence. `unverified` keeps the candidate available for further
evidence; `no_change` or `regression` closes that formation without declaring
the future relation impossible. The producer cannot self-approve. A reviewed
delta may become active only when
its sources, evidence, invalidator, reentry condition, result classification,
and supersession relation are explicit. `no_change`, `regression`, and
`unverified` can remain useful history; they do not become active capability.

Assimilation requires a later non-identical movement to change without the
same correction being reconstructed. File presence, schema validity, review
text, and one successful example are weaker claim levels.

## Stop and openness

Do not recurse into competence-for-competence by default. Add or refine a level
only while another pass changes a material relation. Stop and produce the useful
result when the field is sufficient. Keep latent compositions and retained
unknowns reachable without treating unexercised possibilities as failures.

Retirement removes a competence from current routing; it does not declare the
future relation impossible. Supersession preserves the causal trace without
making history current authority.

## Durable state

`.maios/competences/INDEX.json` owns project-local competence dispositions.
Each admitted event must include a stable id, competence id, work relation,
source references, expected and observed delta, evidence references,
invalidator, reentry condition, review, and any superseded event. The runtime
may validate this shape and current-state concurrency; it cannot decide semantic
quality, approve itself, or grant external effect authority.

`.maios/state/OPERATING_STATE.json` owns formation candidates before admission.
`competence-candidates` exposes their formation, exercise status, evaluation,
and any pending delta. Formation changes the reachable field; it does not add
an entry to the reviewed competence index. Only `admit-competence-delta` can do
that after a separate accepted review.

An optional self-improvement assessment belongs to the reviewed terminal
resultant, not to automatic competence promotion. It identifies a target kind,
id and owner, evidence, uncertainty, expected delta, method readback, and
candidate reference. `improve` additionally requires a formation candidate and
at least one relation through which it enters the next movement. Unresolved
evidence uses `verify_first`. Admission records and routes the candidate for
exercise but never activates, propagates, or freezes the proposed form.
Meta-competences and meta-skills remain eligible for the same revise,
supersede, retire, or reform relation when later context changes.
