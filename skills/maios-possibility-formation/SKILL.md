# Form the possibility offered by the kernel

A possibility can be a different question, method, source, tool, relation, result or organization. It need not be an option displayed to a person. This competence understands how the present field and the participating competences make such a possibility recognizable. KA and FDLA remain inside that formation, not checks appended to an already selected solution.

## Begin with the present, including what is indeterminate

Use the actual operator relation, sources, accumulated reasons and domain knowledge. An object or next action not yet determined does not establish absent context. Retain a clear request as a real determination. Where the work asks what can become possible, let complementary understanding contribute before the first topic filter defines the whole field.

Read a different source family or native index when the current view hides a consequential relationship. A catalogue is a locator, not a limit on composition. The purpose is neither to load every source nor to demand a demonstrated benefit from a relationship before understanding it.

## Illuminate by changing a consequential coordinate

Identify what the first framing presupposes and which relation remains unexplained. A different observer, scale, time, role, source or level may change what can be understood. Let that change revise the question and composition, keeping the actual source distinct from your interpretation. These coordinates are examples, not a list to complete.

In kernel work, counting delivered files can obscure whether the selection retained the capacity to form future methods. Changing the object of inspection to generative inheritance makes another decision possible. The existing successful file checks remain valid for their original question.

## Form a bridge without erasing differences

When two meanings or methods appear opposed, understand their source and conditions. They may be compatible at different scales, may reveal an unrepresented third relation, or may remain genuinely incompatible. Explore the shared function when it makes a useful movement possible; preserve the difference when fusion would falsify either side.

A bridge between a conversational surface and a coding host can concern shared knowledge and continuation. It does not make their tools, local files or authorization identical. A meaningful bridge changes access, understanding or work; a shared label alone does not supply it.

## Read the horizon in the current choice

Consider what the proposed movement opens, preserves, constrains or eliminates for the following field when that difference is material. The future relation contributes to a decision now through its reasons and conditions. It is not an already observed outcome or a predictor required by the kernel.

For example, a source reader with configurable native entry names can preserve access to future competence forms without forcing a new file convention. Its value may justify delivery before a failure, while its invocation remains optional. A current unavailable tool may limit one effect without withdrawing the possibility or independent work.

## Make the result usable

Recompose the newly understood relation with the original sources and still-valid decisions. Return the appropriate result: a clearer question, a chosen design, a useful action, a set of materially different directions, a changed method or an explicit unresolved relation. Do not generate alternatives just to display creativity, and do not rank uncertain possibilities with fabricated precision.

In a human encounter, cooperate with context, domain and expression competences. Recognition cues can make an unfamiliar possibility visible; the person's correction can change the field. A selection is not automatically consent, truth, a personal profile or authorization. An exact existing intent can proceed without another menu.

## Teach the method that formed the possibilities

When a new source or result reveals that the question, filter, comparison criterion or preferred representation caused the loss, revise that method as well as the proposed output. A successful result can also teach a new composition. Domain knowledge remains with its owner; this competence retains the general relation that makes a later non-identical question more capable.

Use the existing kernel continuity relation rather than adding a parallel option registry or a permanent deliberation loop.
