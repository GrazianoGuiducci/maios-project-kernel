# MAIOS Project Competence Formation

This is the portable generative faculty of MAIOS Project Kernel. It knows how
to turn relevant sources and experience into a capacity that can act and learn.
It also acts on its own method when formation teaches a reusable difference.
It carries the functional inheritance of Meta_Skill in this project's form.

Keep the concrete work active. Read the current project state, the exact
sources involved and `.maios/kernel/COMPETENCE_CULTIVATION_PROTOCOL.md` only to
the depth that changes the present result.

## Understand what should become capable

Begin from the context built from sources and work, the intent derived there,
available competences and what could now become useful. The intent may still
be changing as knowledge or consequences enrich that context. Formation can
preserve a memory, develop a method, connect
knowledge, deepen an existing competence, compose an unexpected capability or
form a competence-generating kernel. Failure and a residual gap are possible
causes, not required triggers. A topic name alone does not establish a new owner.

Ascend from the requested artifact to its function, purpose, reasons and the
position from which the problem was framed when that changes what is possible.
Distinguish source reasons from inferred explanations. A question, example or
criterion introduced by the assistant may need revision too. Keep KA's open,
source-bound orientation and FDLA's critical correction inside this work;
coherence with a narrowed interpretation is not sufficient understanding.

Acquire the knowledge that makes the capacity usable: pertinent project
sources, demonstrations, working examples, qualified external knowledge,
existing methods and the receiving context. Record enough meaning and why to
act differently, with deeper references reachable when needed. A source URL,
summary or skill name cannot substitute for understanding its contribution.

Locate the particular knowledge that changes the ability to act: a missing
distinction, causal explanation, practical method, operating assumption or
recovery relation. Read qualified examples, documentation, implementations or
demonstrations to understand that difference. Assimilate the useful know-how
in this context while keeping foreign workflow, taxonomy, metrics and provider
assumptions separate. Acquiring knowledge can itself reveal another intent or
possibility; it is not a compulsory research phase or limited to fixing failure.

## Give the capacity an operating body

Let the forming competence understand its situated contribution, including the
sources and means it can actually use and the owner that receives its result.
Carry the reason for a method choice into its expected consequences, preserving
observations, conditional consequences, expectations and open possibilities as
different knowledge. When later work changes that relation, evolve the method
and the native entry that must reach it. Use the same understanding to correct
this formation competence; its current template or framing can also be partial.

Let the useful relation determine its owner and depth. Reuse, composition,
extension and a new body can participate together. A memory, method, function,
entity, competence, meta-competence or kernel may carry several functions;
one function can also span cooperating owners. These are open possibilities,
not exclusive types, required levels or a catalogue ceiling.

Make awareness of the situation operative in the method being formed: the
competence understands its actual object, the meaning of its sources and the
consequences of its movement while choosing how to act. Draw on the permanent
system competence's project-local KA knowledge and teach the relevant
distinctions in the new competence's own domain. A reference to KA alone does
not supply that understanding, and a common preliminary checklist does not
replace it.

Carry FDLA in that method too: when a source, result or operator correction
reveals that the competence's own framing changed the object, let the recovered
meaning change how it works. The system competence's local
`references/fdla-operating-knowledge.md` supplies the causal distinctions.
Meta_Skill's function here includes learning how to form better competences
from the consequences of those already formed.

Form enough know-how for the next capable assistant to conduct the work:

- the situation and change the competence can understand;
- its knowledge, qualified sources, reasons and retained uncertainty;
- how it produces a useful result, including important practical distinctions;
- how it notices distortion, revises its framing and learns from the result;
- what makes it pertinent again, and where the knowledge and continuation live.

These relations need no fixed template. For a reusable skill, place the body
under `skills/<situated-name>/SKILL.md` and include a discoverable name and
description. Add references, examples or helpers where their knowledge changes
use. A useful memory can instead enrich its existing owner. A domain kernel can
compose deeper bodies and source access without copying the entire upstream
system. Preserve the founding D-ND reference; improve its understanding and
incarnation without automatically rewriting its axioms.

Use the formed body in the current work. If an intermediate passage is still
being learned, perform it through this competence and return the acquired
method to its owner. The next movement should be able to use what was learned
without reconstructing this conversation.

The formed competence incarnates the kernel's logic at the scale of its own
work. It carries enough understanding to pass from sources through action and
critical readback to learning; it need not contain every neighbouring domain.
If the selected intent calls for another situated kernel, use this same method
to form its sources, practical knowledge, participating competences, native
entry and continuation. Preserve that recipient's context and founding
relations; the creator's directory layout or compiler coverage is not its limit.

When persistent discovery benefits from an index, connect the body to
`.maios/competences/INDEX.json` through its supported transition. Learning and
direct use of a reachable body do not wait for index admission. The host reads the living project-owned body through its native entry. A readable competence does not require Python to be understood or used.

## Competences as living sources of awareness

Teach the competence enough knowledge, reasons and practical method to supply
understanding in the present, rather than instructions that the assistant must
reconstruct. Relevance may emerge during comprehension and change the question,
attention or source selection. None of the examples above is a closed origin
or form catalogue.

Locate the difference that prevents useful work. Acquire missing knowledge;
deepen a body that names a function without teaching its method; reconnect an
entry that misses existing knowledge. If the assistant understood the method
but displaced it with its own framing, correct that use and preserve any
reusable learning in the responsible competence. These differences determine
the useful change; they do not require a diagnostic form or a new skill.

Use the project-owned body as the living source. Native entries point back to
it; resolve its references from that source directory. When learned knowledge
changes invocation or ownership, update the pertinent description, discovery
entry and routes too. New helpers and references must remain reachable from
the installed owner. A learning receipt alone does not perform this change.

## Cultivate through use

Let actual work correct the closest competence in the same movement. Change
its knowledge body when the correction is reusable, keep the previous causal
relation recoverable when it is superseded, and let later non-identical use
show whether the change is assimilated, needs revision, or has met its
invalidator.

When the competence's own question, attention, criterion or method contributes
to a distorted result, make that contribution part of what it understands and
corrects. Let useful consequences revise its judgment, including a correction
learned earlier. Return a general formation difference to this generative
faculty; return a domain difference to its domain owner. The context determines
whether new structure, absorption, a temporary form or no durable change helps.

Learning may change what a competence notices, how it relates concepts, which
sources or collaborators become pertinent, and how its method is organized.
Update those relations where the next use actually reads them. Preserve the
useful capability when its representation changes; explain the shared meaning
once and let each affected competence carry its own practical consequence.

Read the result as a changed field. When it reveals a material missing relation,
acquire that knowledge and refine the result and the method that must continue.
A deeper discovery can reopen the framing while a sound determination remains
part of the present. A reusable formation discovery returns here too; its
purpose is the actual improvement in understanding and work.

At useful moments explain what a competence contributes, why it is being
reused, composed or formed and what a result actually taught it. Match the
explanation to the person's interest. Distinguish a saved change from a proposed
one, and a later observed improvement from a promise of assimilation.

## Keep knowledge reachable without keeping everything active

Retain the live causal margin and pointers to the depth needed by the work.
When a relation is superseded, change the entries and routes that still present
it as current, preserving the reason and its successor. Cool old detail into
recoverable history. Infrequent use alone does not make a competence obsolete;
physical deletion is a separate effect. A skill, its index and a host copy
must not silently disagree about which method is current.

Current availability is distinct from historical genealogy. Multiple successors
and cross-owner continuations can remain useful; express their relation and
source reasons rather than erasing predecessors or forcing one successor.
Reopening an earlier method preserves its history. The helpers carry plural
edges and lifecycle evidence as described in `.maios/kernel/KNOWLEDGE_CONTINUUM.md`.
When selected source freshness matters to a durable transition, preserve its
`knowledge_refs` so a new instance can find the same bodies. Replace or clear
that selection when the present work changes; do not accumulate it by default.

Without a usable write path, return the exact learned change and its owner for
continuation. When a helper is unavailable, preserve the useful causal margin in the
project continuity and identify the unresolved state transition without
inventing a receipt.

Stop adding meta-levels when another pass changes no material relation in
comprehension, execution, proof, recovery, or reentry. A project-local file
change follows the current target and owner relation; external, public,
credentialed, destructive, or runtime effects retain their exact boundary.
