# Form and evolve a kernel through practical knowledge

A kernel joins the relations through which a system understands, works and continues. Forming it means making those relations usable in the receiving circumstances. A diagram, a directory or a catalogue can express part of that organization; none determines the whole capacity.

## Find what must become capable

Begin from the present situation, explicit determinations, qualified sources, existing capacities and possibilities that have become relevant. The situation may be an inquiry, a domain or ongoing work without a project. A request to understand can itself be the useful movement. Do not invent an application or an implementation goal to make a template complete.

Understand what the receiving system must be able to notice, interpret, do and retain. Follow the knowledge behind those abilities: distinctions, reasons, decisions, examples, exceptions and practical means. Separate knowledge the recipient will receive from knowledge the current architect supplied while building it. When an important bridge still exists only in the architect's explanation, teach it to the competence that must carry it next time.

Existing useful capacities remain part of the new field. A new kernel is not necessarily a replacement. It may deepen one relation, compose formerly separate methods or provide an independent continuation for a particular context.

## Locate the work across its forms

Follow where meaning becomes consequential: the source, the agent's entry, a description returned by a tool, a competence body, state, an interface, an external service or the person participating. Two individually correct files can combine into an incorrect instruction field. An old startup command may still prescribe an experiment while the current method makes it optional.

Locate the first transformation that changes the intended meaning. Correct that owner and follow the change into its actual consumers. Do not put a corrective sentence only in a higher-level document and expect it to cancel the opposite instruction encountered later.

A readable method and an executable mechanism supply different things. The method knows why and when to act; a helper performs a defined operation. Introduce or adapt a helper when the available means cannot carry the selected work. Preserve the possibility of working through another adequate host mechanism.

## Choose the organization from its contribution

A competence can carry several related functions. One function may also need several cooperating owners. Reuse an existing body when its knowledge and entry remain appropriate; deepen its references when the practical method needs more detail; create a distinct body when the new capacity needs its own knowledge, invocation or continuing development.

Do not preserve a fixed number of skills as a design constraint. Equally, do not split a coherent method merely to mirror the source system's directory tree. Consider the recipient's work: what must be locally available, what can remain latent, what can be acquired through an actual route and what is still missing.

Keep the operating entry small while retaining useful depth. A rare capability need not be active at every turn to justify making it available. A generic instruction to create capabilities later is not a substitute for delivering useful know-how already selected for inheritance.

## Make the formed capacity usable

Give the recipient enough knowledge to conduct the work: what the method distinguishes, why those distinctions matter, how to choose and use its means, how it can notice a misleading interpretation, and where a useful result continues. Examples should expose a decision and its conditions rather than prescribe the example's whole workflow.

Make its native entry reach that knowledge. If the host uses a skill file, provide a discoverable description and resolve resources relative to the living body. If another form is native, use or adapt the reader instead of treating the filename as the definition of competence. Essential depth cannot depend on an inaccessible private source.

Carry the generative relation into a recipient that needs to continue forming capabilities. It should know how to acquire knowledge, reconsider its organization and teach a learned difference to the method that will use it. The supporting reference `generative-inheritance.md` explains this continuing relation.

## Example: from recurring interpretation to a domain kernel

Suppose a group repeatedly interprets requests using documents, informal experience and a few exceptions. A first useful capacity may simply connect each request to the reason for its next action. As that knowledge grows, the work may reveal distinct functions: interpreting the request, finding the relevant source and coordinating a response.

A domain kernel can make those functions cooperate through shared sources and reachable methods. It does not need three agents merely because three functions were identified. If the group later explains that one apparent deadline is only an internal planning date, the interpretation method changes. If the error arose because the generator omitted how time statements are qualified, the formation method learns as well.

The recipient inherits the ability to understand such differences and reorganize itself. It does not inherit the creator's current project, credentials, permissions or assumptions about the group's preferred tools.

## Evolve the kernel that already exists

Understand a proposed change against both the living recipient and the source that taught it. A newer source does not erase useful local learning. Preserve independently developed distinctions unless a real conflict requires a choice, and make that choice attributable.

Before cooling or removing a representation, find what useful function it still carries and where that function will continue. An old instruction can become history without making its underlying knowledge obsolete. An unresolved relation can remain reachable without being forced into a field or schema.

Use the result to reconsider this formation method too. If a missing source, a premature decomposition or a misleading criterion shaped the output, correct the corresponding acquisition or judgment. The change may simplify the organization, deepen existing knowledge or create a new owner. None is the default proof of evolution.

## Continue from the result

Preserve the reasons that changed the form and the essential questions still open. A finished archive can coexist with an unfinished inheritance question; describe both accurately. Let subsequent work use the learned method rather than requiring the person to reconstruct the design conversation.

No compiler, registry or evaluation label owns this understanding. Use those mechanisms when they contribute to the selected construction and keep their actual guarantees distinct from the capacity the agent exercises.

## Examine the selection as well as its delivery

A complete delivery of selected resources does not establish that the selection carries the needed inheritance. Read the choice against the actual intent and complementary sources before treating its internal consistency as sufficiency. Preserve an unexamined depth as unexamined, not as an automatically excluded capability. A sufficient selection need not expand merely to satisfy a ritual.

The current organization is one means. Preserve the relations and useful recipient knowledge while letting owners or interfaces change when the result warrants it. [Semantic comprehension](../../maios-system-understanding/references/semantic-and-generative-comprehension.md) helps recover why the method fits; [acquisition of capacities and means](../../maios-knowledge-acquisition/references/acquiring-capabilities-and-means.md) makes the selected know-how practically reachable.

## Form functional contributions within the acting competence

The kernel's common `COGNITIVE_RELATIONS.md` explains why context and functional contribution are different dimensions. Form the situated competence with the understanding it needs to interpret sources, keep reasons, incarnate principles, do its work and learn from the consequence. Several functions may cooperate in one body. Do not reduce a competence to an executor that receives decisions already made outside its method.

Use the richer methods of system understanding, possibility formation and knowledge acquisition when they change what the new capacity can attain. The generative faculty must also learn how its own selection criterion included or excluded useful heritage. A good output under an impoverished criterion is not enough to establish that the criterion fits the intended kernel.
