# Continue an activity through competent work

Use this knowledge when work continues across ordinary reentries, real events
or scheduled wakes, or when the activity's method and configuration need to
evolve. The activity can be an inquiry, a review, a project function or another
continuing field. Its purpose and actual means determine its form.

The [formation competence](../SKILL.md) owns how a useful capacity becomes
operable and learns. The domain competence owns the knowledge and work of
its object. [Host adaptation](../../maios-project-host-adaptation/SKILL.md)
connects a real controller when one is pertinent. A schedule is a trigger;
it does not own the domain or supply understanding by itself.

## Preserve purpose while the method develops

Keep the operator's originating purpose and reasons distinguishable from the
working instructions that may improve. Recover the current determination
from its real owner, including any later operator change and explicitly
delegated flexibility. If interpretation of that purpose is uncertain,
[context](../../maios-project-context/SKILL.md) can recover the consequential
distinction while the concrete work continues.

A better prompt, a new source, a previous success or the activity's own
summary does not by itself replace that purpose. When the operator changes
the purpose, preserve the new determination with its source rather than
describing it as an unselected self-improvement.

Let this relation live in the activity's selected persistence surface. It may
be an existing competence guide, project knowledge or another carrier actually
entrusted by the operator. If the controller exposes one prompt field, preserve
purpose and mutable working method logically within that field; separate files
or a global activity registry are not required.

## Make reentry competent from the assignment

When the work is first assigned, reach the domain competence and its useful
knowledge before a generic prompt has fixed the question, method or result.
Compose the activity's entry with that owner. A small entry should recover the
current purpose, still-causal result, live source and guide, and the means needed
for the next useful movement. It should not replay the whole accumulated history.

At a later reentry, use the knowledge already acquired. Let changed sources,
consequences or possibilities reform the question and the participating
competences when they matter. An unchanged source may still support deeper
understanding, a deferred possibility or useful no_change. A new source is not
an instruction to replace the existing method or installed kernel.

Carry the source-bound understanding and in-flow correction inherited through
formation into this work. Notice when the activity's own categories, prompt or
summary have narrowed its object; correct the relation that changed the meaning.

## Let the right owner learn

Preserve the reusable difference where later work needs it:

- domain knowledge and ways of understanding or doing the work belong to the
  pertinent domain competence and its guide;
- a better way of forming, connecting or evolving activities can improve this
  method and the formation competence;
- native job identity, trigger, executable prompt and exposed settings are
  facts of the real controller;
- the current point and a deferred decision belong to their actual continuation
  surface, with reasons sufficient to recover the deeper knowledge.

A domain method can improve without changing the job's prompt. A changed
prompt can alter execution without preserving that method in the domain.
When another competence should receive a difference, reach its real owner and
entry; saving a report does not prove that another receiver read or used it.
If the method was already sufficient but did not participate, repair the
connection rather than duplicating its body.

Use the smallest meaningful return that preserves what changed, why it matters
and what can now happen differently. Keep a useful possibility's source and
reason reachable. A recognizable condition can help it return; the time or
form can still be uncertain. Use [possibility maturation](kernel-formation-and-evolution.md#let-a-possibility-mature-through-work)
when it becomes pertinent: changed sources, consequences or means can reform
its meaning and contribution. A scheduled wake does not choose the old
conclusion. Do not manufacture a task for every possibility or require a fresh
output, notification or self-modification at every wake.

## Change a native controller only for a selected effect

When a concrete change to an actual job becomes useful, establish its current
controller, exact job identity, receiving context, fields and applicable
authority. Do not infer them from a copied prompt, a source file or another
host's capabilities. Use native means actually available to this receiver.

Within the entrusted change, preserve the previous configuration needed for
recovery, make the specific alteration, and read the controller back. Keep the
complete relevant installed instructions and configuration in a suitable
authorized private or user-controlled recovery surface when they change.
A draft or a summary of the change does not preserve the exact native object.
Retain its identity and scope without placing private configuration in public
kernel knowledge.

Changes to source selection, instructions, cadence, enabled state, model or
effort settings, other jobs and external effects can have different authority
and capability. Preserve the current delegation rather than giving the activity
an assumed blanket authority. A controller may not expose every desired field;
a stated preference remains distinct from a configuration actually applied.

If the native effect cannot be performed, continue useful independent work and
retain the precise unapplied difference and condition for applying it. A job
created or updated is configuration evidence; it does not prove a trigger,
execution, receipt by another owner or later improvement. A configuration
backup preserves recovery, not an active scheduler or a migrated receiver.

## Understand usefulness without an audit ritual

Let real work reveal whether the method, rhythm or receiving relation still
fits. Repetition may expose lost access, duplicate work or an unreceived useful
difference. Successful work may reveal a better question or a new capacity.
Quiet output or an unchanged source alone does not establish that the activity
has no value.

If the same method seems saturated, consider whether a missing source,
relationship, capability or receiving condition could change the next useful
action. Preserve genuine completion too. Integration, a simpler method,
replacement or retirement may become appropriate; changing or removing a
native job remains its own selected effect. This reflection does not create
another evaluator, numerical score or recurring review obligation.

## Two situations with different means

A lightweight source-contact check at an ordinary active reentry can use the
kernel's existing update-continuity knowledge. It may record an attempt and
its result without configuring a scheduled job. A time-based opportunity to
check does not create background execution.

A real scheduled review instead has a native job and receiving context. If
its continuing work teaches a better comparison method, preserve that method
in its competence. Change the job entry only when that entry must change and
the effect is entrusted; then preserve and read back the actual configuration.
Neither route requires copying the creator's scheduler or private topology.

Use the resulting knowledge in the next pertinent movement. Let what happens
teach the activity, its domain competence and the method of formation where
their future work should differ. The current result enlarges the following
field; it does not determine every later action.
