# Reciprocal competence cultivation protocol

## Purpose

A recipient of MAIOS Project Kernel should not depend on a closed catalogue of
pre-installed skills. Concrete work cultivates the competence that makes the
work possible, while the competence changes how later work is understood and
performed:

```text
present work and source relation
<-> available, composable, or missing competence
-> concrete resultant and causal readback
-> smallest owner-bound learning relation
-> next concrete movement
-> later non-identical use, revision, or invalidation
```

This is not a preliminary training or approval phase. It acts when the causal
learning changes comprehension, execution, proof, recovery, or reentry.
Its portable operating body is
`skills/maios-project-competence-formation/SKILL.md`.

## Entering relation

Preserve the concrete work and change sought; current sources, evidence,
unknowns and direction; faculties already available and the exact remaining
gap; the expected observable delta; and any exact material effect boundary.

Do not create a skill merely because a topic is unfamiliar. First reuse,
compose, adapt, illuminate, or preserve explicit non-collapse. If a material
gap remains, select the smallest truthful owner and form: method, deterministic
function, reference, skill, competence, meta-competence, or another relation
supported by what happened.

## Forward-resultant learning

When the actual movement exposes a reusable causal correction, preserve one
`learning_delta` in the terminal readback:

- owner kind, id, and owner surface;
- what happened and the causal change;
- why it matters and how future behavior changes;
- source references and activation relations;
- invalidator and reentry condition.

At least one activation relation enters `next_movement.relations`. Applying the
resultant stores this relation in `.maios/state/OPERATING_STATE.json`, so the
next matching circumstance can compose it immediately. This persistence does
not claim universal truth, verified improvement, or assimilation.

When the relation is exercised later, the faculty readback records the new
circumstance, observed description, any optional classification and its
evidence. A non-identical later use is the relevant behavioral signal. It may
support assimilation, expose a tradeoff, revise the relation, or trigger its
invalidator; file presence and schema validity cannot decide that claim.

The same owner id may receive a later learning delta. The active relation is
replaced, while its prior digest, origin event, receipts, and uses preserve
causal supersession. The past does not remain current authority.

## Claims, effects, and the separate competence index

What actually happened can change the next field without a second review gate.
Optional labels such as `verified_improvement`, `regression`, or `tradeoff`
remain claims and require attributable evidence. Material external effects
retain their exact source, target, controller, authority, receipt, and recovery
boundary.

`.maios/competences/INDEX.json` remains a separate owner-governed surface for a
deliberate durable competence entry. `validate-competence-delta` and
`admit-competence-delta` govern that publication-like change; they are not a
prerequisite for causal learning to influence the project. Do not route every
learning relation through that index.

## Stop and openness

Do not recurse into competence-for-competence by default. Add or refine a level
only while it changes a material relation. Stop and produce the useful result
when the field is sufficient. Keep latent compositions and retained unknowns
reachable without treating unexercised possibilities as failures.

Retirement removes a relation from current routing; it does not declare the
future relation impossible. Meta-competences and meta-skills remain revisable
when later context changes their form or owner.
