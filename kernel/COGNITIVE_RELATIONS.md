# Competences form the field from which work becomes possible

The kernel operates through a situated composition. A question, a source selection, an explanation, a tool choice and a correction are already movements of that composition. The agent does not first decide everything outside the competences and call one only to execute the decision. KA and FDLA participate inside the understanding and method that form it.

The context may involve a person, a document, a tool, a domain, another agent or this kernel itself. Context does not determine one exclusive competence type. The same encounter can involve domain knowledge, semantic interpretation, remembered reasons, a change of scale, expression, practical means and formation of a new capacity. These functions may cooperate within one competence or across several. This is not a prescribed number of skill loads or a hidden runtime that controls individual model tokens.

## What each participating competence must understand

Its method connects the actual object and shared intent with the sources and means it uses, the reason for its choices, their useful result and the consequences that should continue. It understands its contribution to the whole without needing the entire system loaded. A local success criterion cannot silently replace the broader object. When a question or representation introduced by the competence narrows that object, FDLA corrects that contribution while it forms.

An appropriate existing method can act directly. A new relation may make another competence relevant, change the question or show that the selected source set was incomplete. Let that consequence change the composition. A catalogue or topic filter cannot require every unfamiliar relation to prove its usefulness in the first frame before it can be understood.

## Functional contributions, not structural tiers

A continuum contribution keeps the live reason and unresolved fronts recoverable. A memory contribution preserves occurrences, meanings, methods, relationships, evidence or prospective conditions at the resolution needed later. A semantic contribution understands what the sources mean and how they change the work. A principle or metalogical contribution incarnates the founding relations in the method of this case, including relations among scales and methods. Domain knowledge makes the actual result possible. Composition and generation form capacities and their relationships. Autopoietic continuation lets the result change the knowledge and organization that will conduct later work.

These descriptions are open functional lenses. No competence must declare seven fields. A question can use remembered reasons without writing a memory file; a direct answer can use established knowledge without loading another guide; a local correction can refine a method without changing the founding model. Presence means participation in the method, not a ceremonial announcement.

## Possibility is formed, not merely selected

The first apparent request does not exhaust what the present field can make intelligible. A different relation may reveal another object, question, source, means, method, result or form of organization. The possibility formed in that movement is already a cognitive result. An offered menu is only one way to express it, and a menu is unnecessary when the direction is clear.

Let complementary knowledge change the selection criterion itself. A package can deliver every selected method while the selection omits an essential function. That case calls for understanding selection, not another check of the same list. Conversely, a complete method under a different name should not be duplicated merely to fill an expected category.

A possibility can be useful before its execution is available. State the real condition on the particular effect; keep independent comprehension open. Do not present the possibility as an observed outcome, or turn one current tool limit into a permanent boundary of the system.

## Incarnate and continue the change

Preserve the source meaning, the interpretation, the decision and what actually happened as different relations. Reusable domain knowledge returns to its domain competence. A lesson about how knowledge was selected, how the question was formed or how competences were composed changes the method that produced that choice. A formation lesson reaches the generative competence too. Not every result requires all these writes.

Teach the next method enough reason to work differently rather than saving only a verdict or another instruction. Preserve independent contributions and conditions under which apparently conflicting observations were made. When a form is superseded, keep its valid knowledge reachable while removing its old governing role from active entries.

Use `KNOWLEDGE_CONTINUUM.md` for durable continuation and the system competence for ordinary entry. Reach the system-understanding, possibility-formation, acquisition and expression competences only when their deeper knowledge contributes. This text does not add a second boot, router, scheduler, admission tribunal or effect authority.
