# Knowledge that continues through work

MAIOS competences are sources of practical understanding. They retain what the
project has learned, why it matters, how to act and what could change that
understanding. The assistant reads the pertinent knowledge and works through
it; the result can improve that same competence and the way new competences
are formed. Instructions are an entrance into this knowledge, not a substitute
for comprehension.

For example, a project begins with scattered notes and an existing prototype.
The context competence connects those sources and derives a correctable intent.
A design competence produces a useful change. Work may reveal a reusable
distinction, including through success: the assistant teaches it to the owning
competence with its reasons, then keeps the live continuation compact. At the
next session, the assistant can recover both the method and why it exists.
The example illustrates the intended relation; it is not a reported user trial.

Native skill entries point to the living bodies under `skills/`. A knowledge
change belongs in the source body or its references. A change in invocation,
identity or ownership also reaches the pertinent discovery entry. A state
receipt records a transition; it does not write this semantic improvement or
prove that a later assistant used it.

Keep what affects the present in active attention and retain pointers to
deeper sources. Cool obsolete routes with their reason and recovery condition.
Several still-useful learnings can coexist, including for the same competence;
rare use is not evidence of obsolescence. A new situation may change relevance,
the question or the organization of knowledge itself.

The current operator relation and actual sources can correct stored context.
Reentry must recover meaning and reasons, not only paths and status. Presence
in the continuum means this source-bound operational continuity across work
and interruptions; it does not assert subjective awareness or autonomous work
outside the host's actual execution.

Use `FOUNDING_RELATIONS.md` for the qualified A0-A11 reference and
`COMPETENCE_CULTIVATION_PROTOCOL.md` for learning and its durable continuation.
Their forms remain revisable within the founding D-ND reference. The public
[MAIOS Project Kernel source](https://github.com/GrazianoGuiducci/maios-project-kernel)
provides further study through `knowledge/KERNEL.md` and contribution guidance.
If a remote source is unavailable, continue with the available qualified
knowledge and retain any material missing relation.
