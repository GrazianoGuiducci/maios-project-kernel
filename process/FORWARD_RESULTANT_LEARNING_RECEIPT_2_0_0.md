## Entering correction

Competences preserve the quality of later possibilities only when real work can
change what the system does next. The previous operating cycle already moved
resultant data into configuration and reentry, but placed reusable learning
behind review, assessment, formation candidate, later exercise, pending delta,
and admission. That overlay added latency before the causal correction could
participate in the next field.

## Resultant change

`RESULTANT_READBACK` v2 can carry one owner-bound `learning_delta` containing:

- what happened and its causal delta;
- why the relation matters and how future behavior changes;
- closest owner, sources, activation relations, invalidator, and reentry.

`apply-resultant` atomically stores this relation with canonical configuration,
possibility impact, projections, history, and recovery receipts. A matching
later circumstance can compose it immediately. Later non-identical use is
recorded as behavioral evidence without declaring assimilation.

The optional classification of a result, a wider competence-index change, host
activation, and external material effects remain separate claims or effects
with their own evidence or authority. This keeps validation local to what it
actually governs instead of placing it in front of learning.

## Behavioral evidence

The complete source suite passed `19/19` after the change. Its dedicated fixture
demonstrates:

1. a real correction becomes `learning.competence.source-reconciliation`;

4. the state records `later_nonidentical_use_observed` and the exact receipt;
5. no candidate, review, or competence admission is needed for this causal
   persistence.

The suite also preserves stale-context refusal, atomic recovery, deterministic
build/install behavior, host-claim boundaries, and the separate owner-governed
competence-index path.
